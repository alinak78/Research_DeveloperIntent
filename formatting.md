## Author Guide requirements

Use single-column ACM TAPS formatting: `\documentclass[manuscript,screen,review,anonymous]{acmart}`. Initial content is limited to 14 pages, including figures/tables; references are unlimited. One extra page is available for endmatter. Revisions and accepted papers allow 15 content pages. Appendices are outside the limit but optional for reviewers.

Anonymize text, metadata, supplements, and links. Omit identifying endmatter. Include a Generative AI Usage Statement and ethical considerations; keep limitations in the main body. Disclose substantive AI assistance and its version, with human responsibility and verification.

Register a representative title, abstract, focus areas, author list, and archival choice by the abstract deadline. The author list and archival choice then remain fixed. All authors must complete reviewer signup. Check dual-submission eligibility; non-archival status is not a general workaround. At least one accepted-paper author must register, attend, and present. Follow accessibility guidance.
